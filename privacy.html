Privacy policy

StillNorth Forge · effective 6 October 2026

Who we are

StillNorth Forge is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel The Still North. Contact: soothingvibe4k@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

Through Google OAuth, and only for the channel owner's own Google account, the app requests:

  • youtube.upload and youtube: to upload the owner's videos, set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time, and read their status;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, retention) for an internal daily report.

The app never accesses any other user's account or data.

How data is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server run by the app.
  • No data is sold, rented, shared with or transferred to third parties. There are no ads and no tracking on this site.
  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to report on the owner's channel.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings. Revoking it stops all access; on request to the contact above, the locally stored tokens and reports are deleted.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new effective date.
