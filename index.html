StillNorth Forge

The production tool behind the YouTube channel The Still North.

StillNorth Forge is a private tool run by the owner of The Still North, a channel of calm Nordic landscape videos for sleep, focus and relaxation. It renders the videos on the owner's own computer and uploads them, through the YouTube Data API, to that one channel only. It is not offered to, and does not act for, any other user.

What it does with your Google account

Only the channel owner signs in. The tool uploads the owner's videos, sets their title, description, thumbnail and scheduled publish time, reads their status, and reads the channel's own analytics for an internal daily report.
